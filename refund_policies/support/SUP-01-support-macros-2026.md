"Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Furniture has 45 days. Some items, such as electronics and jewelry, do not get loyalty extensions."

"After your item arrives and passes inspection, we start your refund within 3 business days. It typically posts to your card within 5 to 10 business days."

"Items tagged Final Sale when you bought them can't be returned, but if an item is defective or damaged, we'll make it right."

"Opened electronics have a 15% restocking fee (10% for California). Sealed items and defective items have no fee."

"I'm sorry your order arrived damaged. Please tell us within 7 days of delivery, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund."
