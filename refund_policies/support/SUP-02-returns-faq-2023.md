# Returns FAQ

## How long do I have to return an item?
You have 30 days from delivery to return most items. Furniture can be returned within 60 days.

## Is there a fee to return something?
No! Returns are always free with our prepaid label, for every item and every order.

## Do you charge restocking fees?
Electronics that have been opened are subject to a 20% restocking fee. Furniture has no restocking fee.

## Can I return a gift?
Yes. Gift recipients receive a full cash refund with the gift receipt.

## Do loyalty members get longer return windows?
Gold and Platinum members get 90 days on all items, including electronics.

## Can I return swimwear?
Yes, as long as it is unworn.
