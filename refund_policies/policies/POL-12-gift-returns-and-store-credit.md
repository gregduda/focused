# Gift Returns and Store Credit

## Who can return a gift
A gift recipient can return an item that was sent as a gift order (`is_gift_order`) using the **gift receipt code** printed on the gift receipt together with the order number. The recipient does not need the buyer's email address.

## What the recipient receives
Gift returns are refunded as **store credit** equal to the price paid plus the tax on the item, less any applicable fees. There is no 5% bonus and no cash refund. The return window is the normal one for the item, counted from the delivery date of the gift order. Example: a $50.00 item with $4.00 tax, returned with the $7.95 label fee applying, produces $46.05 of store credit.

## The original buyer
The person who bought the gift can still return it for a refund to the original payment method, using the standard process and the email on the order. The gift recipient's store credit and a buyer's refund cannot both be issued for the same item.

## Fees
The usual label fee (POL-05) is deducted from store credit for a gift return unless a waiver applies. Waivers depend on the return reason and the ship-to state, and on the tier recorded on the gift order, not on the recipient's own loyalty status.

## Store credit rules
Store credit does not expire. In California, store credit balances under $10 can be cashed out on request (ST-CA).
