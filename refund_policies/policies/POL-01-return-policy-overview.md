# Return Policy Overview (Policy Version 2026.1)

Juniper & Pine Outfitters ("J&P") is an online-only retailer that ships within the United States. This is the master summary of our return policy. Category, state, seasonal, and loyalty documents modify it; POL-07 explains how they combine.

## Standard return window
Most items can be returned within **30 days of the delivery date**. Some categories differ: furniture and oversized items have 45 days, electronics have 30 days with no loyalty extension, and perishable, custom, and gift card purchases are generally not returnable.

## What can be returned
Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original condition, with tags and original packaging (see POL-03).

## What cannot be returned
- Items marked Final Sale (POL-15)
- Gift cards (CAT-08)
- Perishable food (CAT-05)
- Custom or personalized items, unless defective or not as ordered (CAT-07)
- Opened hygiene and personal-care items, unless defective (CAT-03)

## How a return works
1. The customer contacts support and verifies identity (OPS-02).
2. If the item is eligible, a return authorization (RMA) is created. An RMA is valid for 14 days.
3. The customer ships the item with the prepaid label. Furniture uses freight pickup instead.
4. After the warehouse inspects the item, the refund is initiated (POL-04).

## Policy version
Version 2026.1 replaced the 2024 return policy on January 1, 2026. Older documents, FAQs, and macros that describe different terms are out of date.
