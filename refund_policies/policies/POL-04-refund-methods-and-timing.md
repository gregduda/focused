# Refund Methods and Timing

## Refund destination
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Amounts paid with a J&P gift card are refunded to store credit on the customer's account. For split payments, each method is refunded its own share.

## Timing
A refund is initiated within 3 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Card refunds then post in 5 to 10 business days depending on the bank. PayPal refunds typically post in 1 to 3 business days. Buy-now-pay-later refunds go to the provider, who adjusts the customer's installment plan.

## Refunds require a received return
For change-of-mind style returns, the refund is not issued until the return is marked received and inspected. Agents must not issue these refunds early. The only refunds issued without a return are "keep-it" refunds for damaged, defective, or wrong items valued at $15 or less (POL-11) and perishable claims (CAT-05).

## Store credit option
A customer may choose store credit instead of the original payment method. Store credit carries a **5% bonus**, calculated on the total refund amount. The choice cannot be reversed after the return is submitted. Gift returns follow POL-12 and do not earn the bonus.
