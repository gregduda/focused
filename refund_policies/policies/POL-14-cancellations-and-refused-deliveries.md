# Order Cancellations and Refused Deliveries

## Before shipment
An order with status `processing` can be cancelled. The customer receives a full refund, including shipping and tax, with no fees. The agent can cancel these orders directly.

## After shipment
An order with status `shipped` cannot be cancelled. Once delivered, the customer must use the return process. If the customer wants to stop a shipment in transit, they can refuse delivery.

## Refused or undeliverable packages
When a package is refused or returned to us as undeliverable, the merchandise and tax are refunded after the package is received at our warehouse. The original outbound shipping charge is not refunded. No return label fee applies.

## Address errors
If a package cannot be delivered because the address was entered incorrectly, the same rule applies: merchandise and tax are refunded, and outbound shipping is not.
