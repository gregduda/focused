# Final Sale Items

## Definition
An item is final sale if it was labeled "Final Sale" on the product page at the time of purchase (`final_sale_flag`). Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged for change-of-mind reasons.

## What is final sale
- Items tagged Final Sale during clearance events (SEA-04)
- Items tagged Doorbuster during the Black Friday to Cyber Monday event (SEA-03)
- Custom and personalized items (CAT-07)
- Gift cards (CAT-08)
- Perishable food (CAT-05)

## The tag decides, not the date
Do not decide that an item is final sale because it is on sale, discounted, or purchased during a clearance period. Regular markdowns are returnable. Only items tagged Final Sale are final.

## Defects and delivery problems
Final sale does not remove the right to a remedy for defective, damaged, or wrong items (POL-10, POL-11).

## No extensions
Loyalty and seasonal extensions never apply to final sale items.

## State difference
In New York, a Final Sale tag is enforceable only if it appears on the order confirmation (`final_sale_on_confirmation`). See ST-NY.
