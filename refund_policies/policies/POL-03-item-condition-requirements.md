# Item Condition Requirements

## General condition
Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and unaltered, with tags attached, in original packaging, and with all parts, accessories, manuals, and free gifts included. Items with odors, stains, or pet hair are not accepted.

## Footwear
Footwear may be tried on indoors on a clean surface only. Footwear that has been worn outdoors is not returnable unless it is defective (POL-10).

## How condition is decided
The support agent approves a return based on what the customer reports about the item. The warehouse inspection after receipt is final. If a customer says that a non-defective item has been used, worn, washed, or damaged by them, the item is not eligible for return. The only exception is the outdoor gear Field-Test Guarantee (CAT-09).

## Failed inspection
If a returned item fails inspection, no refund is issued and the item is shipped back to the customer once at no charge. The customer is notified by email. Support agents cannot override an inspection result.
