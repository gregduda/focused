# Promotions, Bundles, and Free Gifts

## Order-level discount codes
Percentage-off and dollar-off codes are spread across the items in the order in proportion to each item's list price. The refund for a returned item is the amount actually paid for it. Example: an order has item A at $60 and item B at $40, with a 20% code. The customer paid $48 for A and $32 for B. Returning A refunds $48 before tax and fees.

## Spend-threshold promotions
Some promotions require a minimum spend, such as "$20 off $100 or more." If a partial return leaves the remaining items below the threshold (measured at list price), the promotion no longer applies. In that case the refund is calculated as:

**Refund = amount paid for the order's merchandise − list price of the items the customer keeps.**

Example: A ($70) and B ($50) total $120, with $20 off, so the customer paid $100. Returning A leaves B ($50), which is below $100. Refund = $100 − $50 = **$50.00** before tax and fees.

If the remaining items still meet the threshold, use the ordinary proration above.

## Free gift with purchase
A free gift must be returned together with the qualifying item. If it is not returned, the gift's listed value (shown on the order) is deducted from the refund.

## Items sold as a set
Items flagged as sets (`is_set`) must be returned complete. A partial return of a set is not accepted unless the product page says the pieces can be returned separately.
