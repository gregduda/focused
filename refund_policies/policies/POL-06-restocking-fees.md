# Restocking Fees

## Where restocking fees apply
Only two categories carry a restocking fee:
- **Electronics** that have been opened: **15%** of the item price paid (CAT-02).
- **Furniture and oversized items** that are opened, assembled, or used: **20%** of the item price paid (CAT-04).

No other category has a restocking fee.

## How the fee is calculated
The fee is a percentage of the price actually paid for the item, after discounts and before tax and shipping.

## When the fee is waived
- The item is sealed or, for furniture, unopened and unassembled.
- The return reason is DEFECTIVE, DAMAGED_IN_TRANSIT, WRONG_ITEM_SENT, or NOT_AS_DESCRIBED.
- The customer is a Peak member (LOY-01).
- The return is an exchange (POL-13).

## State differences
California reduces the electronics fee to 10% (ST-CA). Massachusetts reduces the furniture fee to 15% (ST-MA). A state percentage replaces the base percentage, and waivers are then applied.

## Multiple rules
If more than one rule could apply, the customer pays the lowest applicable amount.
