# Exchanges

## Eligible items
Exchanges are available for size, color, or variant swaps on apparel and footwear, outdoor gear, and home goods. Electronics, jewelry, furniture and oversized items, custom items, and hygiene items are not exchangeable. For those, the customer returns the item under the regular policy and places a new order.

## Terms
- The exchange must be requested within the item's return window.
- The return label is free and no return label fee applies.
- No restocking fee applies.
- If the replacement costs more, the customer pays the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded.

## If the replacement is out of stock
The exchange becomes a normal return with the label fee waived. The refund is issued after the original item is received.
