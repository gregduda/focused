# How the Refund Amount Is Calculated

## The formula
**Refund = item price paid + tax on that item − applicable fees ± promotion adjustments.**

## Item price paid
Use the amount the customer actually paid for the item, after any discounts are prorated (see POL-09).

## Tax
Sales tax on the returned item is refunded in full. Fees are calculated on the pre-tax price.

## Outbound shipping
The original shipping charge is refunded only if:
- the order is cancelled before it ships (POL-14), or
- every item in the order is returned for our-error reasons (DEFECTIVE, DAMAGED_IN_TRANSIT, WRONG_ITEM_SENT, NOT_AS_DESCRIBED).

It is not refunded for change-of-mind returns, partial returns, or exchanges.

## Fees that may be deducted
Return label fee (POL-05), freight pickup fee (CAT-04), and restocking fee (POL-06).

## Rounding
Round to the nearest cent. Percentage fees round half up.

## Examples
- Home goods item, $80.00 paid, $6.40 tax, changed mind, Basic tier, Texas: $80.00 + $6.40 − $7.95 = **$78.45**.
- Opened electronics, $200.00 paid, $16.00 tax, changed mind, Basic tier, Texas: 15% fee is $30.00. $200.00 + $16.00 − $30.00 − $7.95 = **$178.05**. In California the fee is 10% ($20.00), so the refund is $188.05 with the same tax.
