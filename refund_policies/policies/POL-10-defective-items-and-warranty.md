# Defective Items and Warranty

## Defects inside the return window
If an item is defective within its normal return window, the customer may choose a refund or a replacement. No fees are charged, the return label is free, and the usual condition requirements are relaxed as long as the defect was not caused by the customer.

## Defects after the return window
| Category | Coverage after the window | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Apparel and footwear | 90-day workmanship guarantee from delivery | Refund or replacement |
| Electronics | 1-year manufacturer warranty | Repair or replacement only |
| Outdoor gear | 1-year limited warranty | Repair or replacement only |
| Furniture and oversized | 1-year structural warranty | Replacement parts or repair |
| Jewelry, home goods, hygiene | No extended coverage | None after the window |

Wear and tear, misuse, and accidental damage are excluded.

## What the support agent does
- Defects inside the return window, and apparel or footwear workmanship claims within 90 days, can be handled by the support agent.
- Warranty claims that need repair or replacement after the return window are handed to the warranty team as an escalation of type "warranty" (OPS-01). The agent does not promise the outcome.
- Refunds are not available for electronics, outdoor gear, or furniture defects after the return window. Explain the warranty option instead.

## Final sale items
Final sale does not remove defect coverage (POL-15).
