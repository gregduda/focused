# Black Friday and Cyber Monday Doorbusters (2026)

## Dates
Thanksgiving Day through Cyber Monday: **November 26 to November 30, 2026**.

## Rule
Items tagged **Doorbuster** during this event are final sale. They cannot be returned for change of mind and they are not included in the holiday extension (SEA-02), even though the order falls between November 1 and December 24.

## Non-doorbuster items
Items purchased during the same days that are not tagged Doorbuster are ordinary items. They follow their category rules and the holiday extension.

## Defects
Doorbuster items covered by final sale still get remedies for defects, damage, and wrong items (POL-10, POL-11).

## New York
A doorbuster is enforceable as final sale in New York only if "Final Sale" appears on the order confirmation line (ST-NY).
