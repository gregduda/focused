# Holiday Extended Returns (2026)

## Who qualifies
Items ordered between **November 1 and December 24, 2026** (by order date, `order_date`) qualify for extended returns.

## Deadlines
| Category | Return by |
|---|---|
| Apparel and footwear, outdoor gear, home goods, jewelry, sealed hygiene items | January 31, 2027 |
| Electronics | January 15, 2027 |

## Not included
- Furniture and oversized items keep their 45-day window from delivery.
- Perishables, custom items, gift cards, and final sale items.
- Doorbuster items (SEA-03), even when ordered inside the holiday dates.

## How it combines with other windows
The customer gets whichever deadline is later: the holiday deadline or the normal window ending 30 days (or the longer applicable window) after delivery. Example: an item ordered December 20 and delivered January 25 uses the normal 30-day window (ending February 24), because it is later than January 31.

## Return authorization
The return must still be initiated by the deadline. The RMA is then valid for 14 days.

## Important
Loyalty, state, and category exclusions are not removed by this program (POL-07).
