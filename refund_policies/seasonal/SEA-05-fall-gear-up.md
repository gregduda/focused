# Fall Gear-Up Extended Returns (2026)

## Who qualifies
Items ordered between **September 1 and October 15, 2026** (by order date).

## Categories
Apparel and footwear, and outdoor gear only.

## Rule
The return window is **45 days** from delivery instead of 30.

## Not included
Electronics, hygiene items, home goods, jewelry, furniture and oversized items, and all non-returnable categories.

## Stacking
This is not added to other windows. The customer gets the single latest deadline (POL-07). A Summit member gets 45 days, and a Peak member gets 60 days, not 105.
