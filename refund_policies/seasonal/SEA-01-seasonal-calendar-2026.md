# Seasonal Programs Calendar 2026

This calendar summarizes the seasonal programs. The linked document for each program has the full rules and governs if anything differs.

| Program | Dates | Rule | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Summer Clearance | July 6 to August 16, 2026 (ended) | Tagged items are final sale | SEA-04 |
| Fall Gear-Up | Orders placed September 1 to October 15, 2026 | 45-day window for apparel and footwear, outdoor gear | SEA-05 |
| Holiday Extended Returns | Orders placed November 1 to December 24, 2026 | Return by January 31, 2027 (electronics by January 15, 2027) | SEA-02 |
| Black Friday and Cyber Monday Doorbusters | November 26 to November 30, 2026 | Tagged doorbusters are final sale | SEA-03 |
| Florida hurricane season | Deliveries June 1 to November 30 | 37-day window for FL orders | ST-FL |

## Winter Clearance
Winter Clearance for 2027 has not been scheduled yet. As in every clearance, only items tagged Final Sale are final.

## How programs interact
Windows never stack. The customer gets the latest applicable deadline (POL-07).
