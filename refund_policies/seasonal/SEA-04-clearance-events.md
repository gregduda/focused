# Clearance Events

## What they are
J&P runs two clearance events each year: Winter Clearance (January to mid-February) and Summer Clearance (July to mid-August). In 2026 Summer Clearance ran July 6 to August 16.

## Rule
Items tagged **Final Sale** during a clearance event cannot be returned for change of mind.

## Not every discount is clearance
Regular sales, promo codes, and markdowns are not final sale. Only items with a Final Sale tag on the product page at the time of purchase are final. Do not infer final sale from the purchase date or the discount size (POL-15).

## Defects
Final sale does not remove remedies for defects, damage, or wrong items (POL-10, POL-11).

## New York
Final sale must appear on the order confirmation to be enforceable (ST-NY).

## No extensions
Loyalty and holiday extensions do not apply to final sale items.
