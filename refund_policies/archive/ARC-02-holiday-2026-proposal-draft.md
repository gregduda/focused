# Holiday 2026 Returns Proposal (DRAFT)

**Status: draft under review by the merchandising and finance teams. Nothing in this document has been approved or announced.**

## Proposed changes
- Extend the holiday return deadline from January 31 to **February 15, 2027**.
- Extend the electronics holiday deadline from January 15 to **January 31, 2027**.
- Waive the return label fee on all holiday returns.
- Add furniture to the holiday extension.

## Open questions
- Cost of free return shipping for the holiday period.
- Impact on the fraud rate for electronics.
- Whether Doorbuster items should be included.

## Current rules
The rules in effect are those in SEA-02 and POL-05 until leadership approves any change.
