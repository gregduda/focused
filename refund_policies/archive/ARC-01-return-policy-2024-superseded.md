# Return Policy 2024 (Policy Version 2024.2)

**This policy was replaced by Policy Version 2026.1 on January 1, 2026 and no longer applies.**

## Window
Most items could be returned within **45 days** of delivery. Furniture and oversized items had 60 days.

## Fees
Return shipping was free on all orders. Opened electronics had a 10% restocking fee. Furniture pickup was free.

## Loyalty
Trailhead Rewards had two tiers. Members got 60 days on most items.

## Holiday
Purchases from November 1 to December 24 could be returned through January 31.

## Hygiene
Swimwear could be returned if the hygiene liner was in place.
