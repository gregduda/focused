# Apparel and Footwear Returns (category key: apparel_footwear)

## Return window
30 days from delivery. Longer windows can apply: Trailhead Rewards Summit (45 days) and Peak (60 days) members (LOY-01), Fall Gear-Up orders (45 days, SEA-05), Washington deliveries (45 days, ST-WA), and the holiday extension to January 31 (SEA-02). The customer gets the single latest deadline (POL-07).

## Condition
Unworn, unwashed, tags attached, original packaging. Footwear may be tried on indoors only. Footwear worn outdoors is not returnable unless defective.

## Not in this category
Swimwear, underwear, and socks are hygiene items with their own rules (CAT-03).

## Fees
Label fee only (POL-05). No restocking fee.

## Exchanges
Available for size and color swaps (POL-13).

## Defects
90-day workmanship guarantee from delivery for defects in materials or construction such as failing seams, zippers, or separating soles. Remedy is refund or replacement (POL-10).
