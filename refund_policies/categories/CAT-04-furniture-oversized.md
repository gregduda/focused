# Furniture and Oversized Items (category key: furniture_oversized)

Oversized means over 50 lb or longer than 72 inches on the longest side.

## Return window
**45 days** from delivery. Loyalty tiers, Fall Gear-Up, Washington, and the holiday extension do not apply to this category. Because 45 days already exceeds the Florida 37-day window, Florida changes nothing here.

## Freight pickup fee
Furniture is returned by freight pickup rather than a shipping label. A **$75 pickup fee** is deducted from the refund. In Massachusetts the fee is $50 (ST-MA). The pickup fee is waived for our-error reasons. It is **not** waived by loyalty tiers, including Peak, and it is not waived in Illinois.

## Restocking fee
- Opened, assembled, or used: **20%** of the price paid (15% in Massachusetts).
- Unopened and unassembled: no restocking fee.
- Waived for our-error reasons and for Peak members.

## Damage
Report within 7 days of delivery. Furniture damage claims always go to a human for photo review because the value exceeds the keep-it limit (POL-11, OPS-01).

## Defects
1-year structural warranty covering replacement parts or repair (POL-10).

## Exchanges
Not available. Return and reorder.
