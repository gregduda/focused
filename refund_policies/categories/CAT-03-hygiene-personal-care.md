# Hygiene and Personal-Care Items (category key: hygiene_personal_care)

## What counts as hygiene
- Swimwear
- Underwear and undergarments
- Socks
- In-ear earbuds, including those cataloged as electronics
- Cosmetics and skincare
- Razors, grooming tools, and toothbrushes

## Rule
These items can be returned only if they are **sealed and unworn**, with any hygiene liner or seal intact. If the item has been opened, worn, or used, it cannot be returned unless it is defective or the return is for an our-error reason (wrong item, damaged in transit).

## Return window
30 days from delivery for sealed items. Loyalty extensions (LOY-01), the Florida hurricane-season window (37 days, ST-FL), and the holiday deadline of January 31 (SEA-02) can extend the window for sealed items. Washington's 45-day window and Fall Gear-Up do not apply to hygiene items. Extensions never make opened items returnable (POL-07).

## Fees
Label fee only. No restocking fee.

## Defects
A defect inside the return window is handled under POL-10 with a refund or replacement, even for opened items.
