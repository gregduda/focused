# Home Goods and General Merchandise (category key: home_goods)

Home goods include cookware, dinnerware, décor, bedding, pillows, and small appliances.

## Return window
30 days from delivery. Loyalty tiers (LOY-01), Washington (45 days, ST-WA), Florida hurricane season (37 days, ST-FL), and the holiday deadline of January 31 (SEA-02) can apply. Fall Gear-Up does not apply to home goods.

## Condition
Unused and in original packaging. Small appliances that have been plugged in or used are not returnable unless defective. Bedding and pillows must be in sealed or original packaging.

## Breakage
Glass, ceramic, and other breakables that arrive broken are handled under POL-11.

## Fees and exchanges
Label fee only, no restocking fee. Exchanges are available for variants (POL-13).

## Defects
Defects inside the return window are handled under POL-10. There is no extended coverage after the window.
