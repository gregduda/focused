# Jewelry Returns (category key: jewelry)

## Return window
30 days from delivery. **Loyalty tiers do not extend the jewelry window**, and Fall Gear-Up and Washington's 45-day window do not apply. The holiday deadline of January 31 (SEA-02) and the Florida 37-day window (ST-FL) do apply.

## Condition
Unworn, with tags, in the original box, with any certificates or appraisals included. Engraved or resized pieces are custom items and cannot be returned (CAT-07).

## Escalation for high-value items
Any single jewelry item with a price paid of **$500 or more** must be escalated to a human for verification before a return is authorized (OPS-01). The agent does not approve these returns on its own.

## Shipping
Jewelry returns ship with an insured, signature-required label. Summit and Peak members still get the label fee waived (LOY-01).

## Fees
Label fee only. No restocking fee.

## Exchanges
Not available. Return and reorder.
