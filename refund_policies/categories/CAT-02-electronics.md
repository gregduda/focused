# Electronics Returns (category key: electronics)

## Return window
30 days from delivery. **Loyalty tiers do not extend the electronics window**, and Fall Gear-Up does not apply. Washington's 45-day window does not apply. Electronics purchased in the holiday period have a January 15 deadline (SEA-02). Florida hurricane-season deliveries get 37 days (ST-FL).

## Restocking fee
- Opened electronics: **15%** of the price paid (10% in California, ST-CA).
- Sealed, unopened electronics: no restocking fee.
- No restocking fee for our-error reasons or for Peak members (POL-06).

## Requirements
Return all accessories, cables, and manuals. Sign out of all accounts and factory reset the device.

## In-ear earbuds
In-ear earbuds are treated as hygiene items even when the catalog lists them as electronics. Opened in-ear earbuds cannot be returned unless defective. See CAT-03.

## Defects
Defective inside 30 days: refund or replacement with no fee. After 30 days and up to 1 year: manufacturer warranty claim for repair or replacement, not a refund (POL-10).

## Exchanges
Not available. Return and reorder.
