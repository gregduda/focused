# Gift Cards (category key: gift_card)

## Rule
Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded, including when purchased by mistake. They have no cash value.

## State exception
In California, a gift card or store-credit balance under $10 can be cashed out on request (ST-CA).

## Unauthorized purchases
If a customer reports that a gift card was purchased without their authorization, escalate to a human (OPS-01). The agent does not refund gift cards.

## Lost or stolen cards
A card can be replaced only if it was registered to the customer's account. Unregistered cards are treated like cash.
