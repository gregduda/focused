# Custom and Personalized Items (category key: custom_personalized)

Custom items include engraved, monogrammed, embroidered, made-to-order, and custom-dimension products.

## Rule
Custom items are final sale. They cannot be returned for change of mind, fit, or ordering mistakes.

## Exceptions
A custom item can be returned or remade if it is defective, damaged in transit, or not as ordered because of a production error, meaning the result differs from what the customer submitted. The customer must report this within 30 days of delivery. The remedy is a remake or a refund, at the customer's choice, with a free return label if the item must come back.

## What is not a production error
A spelling mistake or wrong text that the customer typed themselves is not a defect. The item stays final sale.

## Extensions
No loyalty, seasonal, or state extensions apply.
