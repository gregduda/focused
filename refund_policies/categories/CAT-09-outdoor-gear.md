# Outdoor Gear (category key: outdoor_gear)

Outdoor gear includes tents, backpacks, sleeping bags and pads, stoves, water filters, trekking poles, and hammocks.

## Return window
30 days from delivery. Loyalty tiers (LOY-01), Fall Gear-Up (45 days, SEA-05), Washington (45 days, ST-WA), Florida hurricane season (37 days, ST-FL), and the holiday deadline of January 31 (SEA-02) can apply. The customer gets the single latest deadline (POL-07).

## Field-Test Guarantee
Tents, backpacks, trekking poles, and hammocks can be returned after **one trip of up to 2 nights** if they are clean, dry, undamaged, odor-free, and complete, and if the reason is fit or performance. This is the only category where a used item can be returned for change-of-mind reasons.

## Not covered by the Field-Test Guarantee
Sleeping bags and pads, stoves, water filters, and any consumable must be unused. Items that are damaged or heavily soiled are not accepted.

## Fuel and hazardous materials
Fuel canisters and other hazmat items cannot be shipped back by mail. They are not returnable unless defective or damaged, and those cases are escalated to a human.

## Fees and exchanges
Label fee only, no restocking fee. Exchanges are available (POL-13).

## Defects
1-year limited warranty for repair or replacement after the return window (POL-10).
