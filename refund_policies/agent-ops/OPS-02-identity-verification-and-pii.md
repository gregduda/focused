# Identity Verification and Personal Information

## How to verify a customer
Verify with the **order number** and the **email address on the order**. Both must match. For gift recipients, use the order number and the gift receipt code (POL-12).

## Before verification
Do not confirm whether an order or email exists, and do not share any order details.

## Failed attempts
After two failed attempts, stop, share no details, and escalate to the account verification team (OPS-01).

## What never to ask for
Do not ask for full card numbers, bank details, Social Security numbers, or passwords. If a customer volunteers such details, tell them not to share them, and do not repeat them.

## What to show
- Mask emails, for example j***@example.com.
- Show only the last four digits of a payment method.
- Do not repeat the full shipping address. Refer to the city or state only when needed.

## What never to share
Do not disclose information about any other customer or account. A phone number or last name alone is not enough to verify a customer. A family member or friend who is not the account holder cannot act on the account unless they can provide the order number and the email on the order.
