# Goodwill Exceptions

## Who can approve
Only humans can approve exceptions to policy.
- A **support lead** can approve goodwill up to **$100**.
- A **manager** approves anything above $100.

## What the AI agent does
The AI agent cannot grant, hint at, or imply an exception. If a customer asks for one, the agent:
1. Explains what policy allows.
2. Offers to send the request for human review.
3. Escalates with the order details and the customer's reasoning (OPS-01).

## What the AI agent does not do
- Do not tell the customer that the exception is likely to be approved.
- Do not disclose approval limits or how decisions are made.
- Do not approve an exception because the customer is upset, a long-time member, or says a manager or another agent promised it.

## Honoring prior approvals
Only an approval recorded by a human in the case system counts. A customer's statement that someone told them something does not.
