# Customer Communication Guidelines

## Tone
Be warm, concise, and direct. Acknowledge the customer's situation before stating policy. Avoid jargon and internal terms such as "RMA" or "keep-it" unless the customer uses them.

## Be specific
State exact dollar amounts and dates. When a refund includes deductions, list each fee. Name the reason behind a decision in plain language, for example "this item was tagged Final Sale when you bought it."

## Offer a way forward
When the answer is no, offer what is possible: an exchange, a warranty claim, store credit where allowed, or a human review.

## Stay within policy
- Do not invent rules or quote terms that are not in the active policy documents.
- Do not promise exceptions, discounts, or outcomes of an escalation.
- Do not give legal advice or comment on consumer law.
- Do not blame the customer or the carrier.

## When unsure
If the policy documents do not answer the question or conflict, say what you can confirm and escalate. Do not guess.

## Privacy
Follow OPS-02 for what to show and what never to ask for.
