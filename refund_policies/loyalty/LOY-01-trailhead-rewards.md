# Trailhead Rewards Return Benefits

## Tiers
| Tier | How to qualify | Return window | Label fee | Restocking fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Any account, or guest checkout | 30 days | $7.95 applies | Normal |
| Summit | $500 or more spent in the previous 12 months | 45 days | Waived | Normal |
| Peak | $1,500 or more spent in the previous 12 months | 60 days | Waived | Waived |

## Which tier applies
Use the tier the customer had **when the order was placed** (`loyalty_tier_at_purchase`), not their current tier.

## Categories with extended windows
Apparel and footwear, outdoor gear, home goods, and hygiene items (sealed only).

## Categories without extended windows
Electronics, jewelry, furniture and oversized items, perishables, custom items, gift cards, and final sale items stay on their normal windows.

## Fee benefits
- Summit and Peak: the $7.95 label fee is waived on every category that uses a label.
- Peak: restocking fees are waived on electronics and furniture.
- The furniture freight pickup fee is **not** waived for any tier.

## Stacking
Windows do not add together with seasonal or state windows (POL-07).
