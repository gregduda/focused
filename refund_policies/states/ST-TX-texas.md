# Texas Addendum (ship-to state: TX)

There are no Texas-specific changes. Orders shipped to Texas follow the standard return policy, including the standard 30-day window, the $7.95 label fee, and 15% and 20% restocking fees.

Do not apply terms from other state addenda to Texas orders.
