# California Addendum (ship-to state: CA)

## Electronics restocking fee
For orders shipped to California, the restocking fee on opened electronics is **10%** of the price paid, instead of 15%. Waivers still apply (sealed items, our-error reasons, Peak members, exchanges).

## Small balances
A store-credit or gift card balance under **$10** can be cashed out on request. This applies to balances held by a California customer.

## Everything else
All other terms follow the standard policy.
