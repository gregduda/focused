# Massachusetts Addendum (ship-to state: MA)

## Furniture and oversized items
For orders shipped to Massachusetts:
- The freight pickup fee is **$50** instead of $75.
- The restocking fee on opened, assembled, or used items is **15%** instead of 20%.

Waivers still apply: our-error reasons waive both fees, and Peak members are exempt from the restocking fee but still pay the pickup fee.

## Everything else
All other terms, including electronics fees, follow the standard policy.
