# Washington Addendum (ship-to state: WA)

## Extended window
For orders shipped to Washington, the return window is **45 days** from delivery for these categories:
- Apparel and footwear
- Outdoor gear
- Home goods

## Not included
Electronics, jewelry, furniture and oversized items, hygiene items, and every non-returnable category are not extended. Hygiene items still require sealed condition.

## Stacking
This is not added to loyalty or seasonal windows. The customer gets the single latest deadline (POL-07). A Peak member in Washington gets 60 days, not 105.

## Everything else
All other terms follow the standard policy.
