# New York Addendum (ship-to state: NY)

## Final sale disclosure
For orders shipped to New York, an item tagged Final Sale is final only if the words "Final Sale" appear on that item's line on the **order confirmation** (`final_sale_on_confirmation`). If the confirmation does not show it, the item is treated as a regular item and can be returned under its category rules, including the standard window.

## What this changes
This applies to clearance and doorbuster items (POL-15, SEA-03, SEA-04). It does not change categories that are non-returnable by their nature, such as perishables, custom items, gift cards, and opened hygiene items.

## Everything else
All other terms follow the standard policy.
