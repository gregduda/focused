# Florida Addendum (ship-to state: FL)

## Hurricane-season window
For orders shipped to Florida and **delivered between June 1 and November 30**, the return window is **37 days** from delivery for every category that has a return window.

## How it stacks
This is not added to other windows. The customer gets the single latest deadline (POL-07). Examples:
- A Basic-tier customer buying apparel gets 37 days.
- A Summit member gets 45 days, because 45 is later than 37.
- Furniture keeps its 45-day window.

## Timing rule
Only the delivery date matters. An order delivered on May 31 does not qualify, and an order delivered on June 1 does.

## Non-returnable categories
Perishables, custom items, gift cards, and final sale items stay non-returnable.

## Everything else
All other terms follow the standard policy.
